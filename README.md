# Abalone Genus
### Polymath · Interdisciplinary Thinker · Lifelong Learner

Curious about the world, driven by questions, and fascinated by the
connections between disciplines.

I have a deep appreciation for understanding how things work,
how ideas emerge, and how knowledge can be translated into
meaningful contributions.

My interests span several fields, including:

- **Science & Physics** — Exploring the fundamental principles
  that govern nature and the universe.
- **Engineering & Technology** — Understanding systems,
  designing solutions, and turning ideas into reality.
- **Astronomy & Cosmology** — Exploring the universe, celestial
  phenomena, and humanity's place within the cosmos.
- **Psychology** — Studying human behavior, cognition,
  perception, and the complexity of the mind.
- **Theology & Philosophy** — Examining questions of existence,
  meaning, faith, reason, and the nature of knowledge.
- **Entrepreneurship** — Exploring innovation, building ventures,
  and transforming ideas into practical impact.

### A Multidisciplinary Perspective

I believe that knowledge becomes more powerful when disciplines
intersect. The principles of physics can inspire engineering,
psychology can inform entrepreneurship, and philosophy can
challenge the assumptions behind scientific inquiry.

Rather than limiting curiosity to a single domain, I value
exploring different perspectives and discovering connections
between seemingly unrelated fields.
